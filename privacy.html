Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Home Calendar is a personal Raspberry Pi display used only by the owner. This policy describes the only Google data it requests.

Data used

The app requests read-only access to Google Calendar (calendar.readonly). It reads event titles, times, locations, and calendar names so they can be shown on the home screen.

How it is stored

Events are fetched on the Pi and kept in memory for a few minutes so the screen can refresh. A Google OAuth token is stored only on that Pi so the display can keep reading the calendar. Nothing is sent to an advertising network, analytics service, or other company.

Sharing

Calendar data is not sold, rented, or shared. It is shown on the household display and can be spoken aloud on that display’s speakers if the owner turns announcements on.

Retention and deletion

Disconnect the app in Google Account, Security, Third-party access, and delete token.json on the Pi. That stops further access. Cached events are discarded when the Pi restarts.

Contact

Questions go to the Gmail address listed as the support email on the Google consent screen.
